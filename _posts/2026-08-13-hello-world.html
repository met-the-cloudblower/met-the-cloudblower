Welcome to something… new.

We wanted a room that felt like a strange little broadcast from the edge of the night: dark, warm, bright notes, and enough atmosphere to feel alive without becoming loud.

This is where we keep the odd little stories, the soft observations, the sharp thoughts, and the rituals of living a weird life in a very ordinary world.

Met is the dom owner. Pop is the sub puppy. The blog is part diary, part theatre, part signal from the underglow.
